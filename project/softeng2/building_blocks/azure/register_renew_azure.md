# Register and renew your Azure for Students subscription

## How to register or renew

To renew **Azure for Students**, you usually renew by **signing up again for the Azure for Students offer** after your 12-month term ends or near your anniversary date. Microsoft says students can renew annually as long as they’re still eligible.

Link: https://azure.microsoft.com/free/students/

"Get started with Microsoft Azure for Students and enjoy access to cloud services, developer tools, and $100 credits to build apps, explore AI and data science."

### Steps

1. Go to the **Azure for Students** page. Click the `Start free` button!
2. Sign in with the **same Microsoft account** you used before, preferably connected to your university email.
3. Click **Sign up** or **Apply now**.
4. Complete **student verification** using your university email.
5. After approval, check your subscription in:
   **Azure Portal → Subscriptions**
6. Check your renewed credit in the **Azure Sponsorships / Credits** page.

Azure for Students normally gives **$100 credit for 12 months**, and renewal requires you to still be a full-time student at an eligible institution and verify with your school email.

### If you don’t see a renew button

That can happen. Microsoft’s FAQ says renewal is done by signing up for the Azure for Students offer again, not always through a visible “Renew” button in the portal.

### If it says “not eligible to renew”

Common reasons:

- Your 12-month term has not ended yet.
- Your student verification failed or your school email is no longer valid.
- You are not recognized as a full-time student.
- The subscription was converted, canceled, or eligibility lapsed.

If your subscription is disabled because the credit expired or was used up, Microsoft has a separate reactivation guide for Azure for Students subscriptions.

Best next move: try renewing from the **Azure for Students website** using your university email, then check **Azure Portal → Subscriptions**.

> [!TIP]
>
> Log out and log back again to the Azure Portal in order to make your new credits appear.

## Azure Subscription Models

Azure services are available under several pricing models:

- **Azure for Students**: an offer aimed at students. As long as you are a student, Microsoft provides an annual credit of $100 USD.
- **Azure free account** for those registering for the first time. Every new user can spend $200 USD over 12 months. More details [here](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/search/?&ef_id=Cj0KCQiArvX_BRCyARIsAKsnTxNdf0D2Zw-Vym1piGbIiyCUva1ICRrl6I3aIA46TOgVPUAk7JF0hX8aApBtEALw_wcB:G:s&OCID=AID2100641_SEM_Cj0KCQiArvX_BRCyARIsAKsnTxNdf0D2Zw-Vym1piGbIiyCUva1ICRrl6I3aIA46TOgVPUAk7JF0hX8aApBtEALw_wcB:G:s).
- **Pay-as-you-go**: credit card details are required, and Microsoft charges the cost of consumed services on a monthly basis. Card details must be provided even if you only use free services and no billable costs are incurred.
- **Enterprise agreement**: large, enterprise-level customers sign a separate contract. Above a certain resource demand, the Pay-as-you-go model is no longer available. Pricing is also a matter of negotiation in this tier.

## My Azure for Students subscription isn't working… What can I do?

You can register for a 12-month **Azure free account** [here](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/purchase-options/azure-account/). In this case, a credit card number is required — Microsoft will charge a small amount in dollars to verify its validity, which will later be refunded. You need to be very careful here, as any services used will be billed without exception. However, with a little attention, costs can be kept low or avoided altogether.

Anyone can sign up for a **Pay-as-you-go** subscription. Here too, billing is based on the services consumed.
